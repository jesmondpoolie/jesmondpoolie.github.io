I am a **Chief Cardiac Scientist** based in Edinburgh, Scotland, working within NHS Lothian and conducting postgraduate research at **Edinburgh Napier University**. 

My work bridges cardiology, healthcare experience research, and modern computational data workflows. My doctoral research investigates adult patient experiences of non-invasive diagnostic cardiac imaging, examining how clinical pathways, patient-clinician interactions, and diagnostic environments shape the overall care experience.

---

### 🔬 Research & Clinical Focus

- **Cardiovascular Diagnostics:** Clinical delivery, protocol design, and quality governance across non-invasive diagnostic cardiac modalities.
- **Qualitative Health Methodologies:** Rigorous synthesis of patient experience using **Braun & Clarke's Reflexive Thematic Analysis**, alongside evidence synthesis following the **JBI Scoping Review** framework and PRISMA-ScR guidelines.
- **Healthcare Data & Pipelines:** Developing reproducible data extraction, screening, and analysis workflows using Python, R, NVivo, and Covidence.

---

### 💻 Computing, Systems & Data

Alongside clinical practice, I maintain and explore self-hosted computing infrastructure and open-source analytical tools:

- **Data Wrangling & Analysis:** Automating data transformation, reporting, and multicriteria decision-making (MCDM) algorithms using Python (`pandas`, `numpy`) and R (`tidyverse`).
- **Knowledge Architecture:** Constructing structured, interlinked research vaults in Obsidian to manage literature notes, reflexivity journals, and diagnostic governance protocols.
- **Local AI & Inference:** Hosting and evaluating local Large Language Models via `llama.cpp` and LiteLLM pipelines to support literature synthesis and qualitative familiarisation workflows on private, self-contained hardware.
- **Self-Hosted Infrastructure:** Administering secure Linux systems, Docker microservices (Nextcloud, Syncthing), and edge networks secured via Tailscale.
